# Landlord Workflow: From Onboarding → Tenant Equity → Exit  
### Plain Language, End-to-End Guide

This file summarizes the full landlord workflow used by KeyPath.  
Everything here is safe, public-facing content appropriate for the AI assistant.

It explains how a landlord activates a home on KeyPath, how tokenized equity works, and how exits are handled.

---

# OVERVIEW

Total landlord time required: **20–30 minutes**  
KeyPath handles everything else.

KeyPath does NOT:
- Take title  
- Affect the mortgage  
- Interfere with lender rights  
- Change rent  
- Approve tenants  
- Replace property management  
- Own customer data  
- Require new software systems

---

# 1. Landlord Onboarding (Steps 1–9)

## Step 1 — Provide Basic Property Information  
Landlord submits:
- Property address  
- Unit details (beds, baths, home type)  
- Photos  
- Property tax data (optional)  

KeyPath uses this to:
- Pre-qualify the property  
- Set up initial valuation parameters  
- Create the digital ledger entry

---

## Step 2 — Provide Property Valuation Inputs  
Landlord chooses one:
- Upload a recent appraisal  
- Upload a CMA or BPO  
- Let KeyPath run automated valuation (AVM blend)

This sets the **Initial Token Value**.

---

## Step 3 — Ownership & Equity Verification  
Landlord uploads:
- Deed or title  
- Mortgage statement  
- Any liens/HELOCs  
- Tax status  

KeyPath uses this to confirm:
- Total property value  
- Total debt  
- Verified landlord equity  

KeyPath generates:
- Anti-Dilution Rule  
- Refinance Safety Firewall  
- Equity Stability Clauses  

---

## Step 4 — Choose How Much Equity to Tokenize  
Landlord selects:
- % of their equity to allocate to the Tenant Token Pool  
- Total number of Tokens  
- Buyback rules  
- Annual valuation rules

Typical pilots tokenize 10–30% of landlord equity.

---

## Step 5 — Define Tenant Accrual Rules  
Landlord sets:
- Monthly contribution options  
- On-time rent incentives  
- Maintenance incentives  
- Renewal incentives  
- Late rent penalties (for Token accrual only)

These rules govern how tenants earn Tokens.

---

## Step 6 — Set Buyback & Exit Preferences  
Landlord defines:
- First Right of Refusal  
- Maximum equity tenant can earn annually  
- Buyback discount (if any)  
- Whether KeyPath marketplace is enabled  

This governs liquidity and exit options.

---

## Step 7 — Add Units & Tenants  
Landlord:
- Adds tenants manually or via import  
- Uploads leases  
- Confirms rent amounts  
- Sends tenant invitations (email or SMS)

Once tenants join, their dashboard activates.

---

## Step 8 — KeyPath Creates the Protected Series Structure  
Depending on landlord setup:

### If property is already in an LLC:
- KeyPath creates a **Protected Series** in the existing LLC  
- No title transfer required  

### If property is owned personally:
- KeyPath helps create:
  - A new umbrella LLC  
  - A Protected Series under that LLC  

Landlord pays state filing and Series LLC fees.

This structure protects tenant equity from:
- Refinancing  
- Bankruptcy  
- Liens  
- New investors  

---

## Step 9 — KeyPath Activates the Platform  
KeyPath sets up:
- Digital ledger (off-chain)  
- Landlord dashboard  
- Tenant dashboard  
- Token accrual engine  
- Rent reporting  
- Annual valuation cycle  

The property is now fully live.

---

# 2. Tenant Onboarding (Steps 10–15)

## Step 10 — Tenant Creates Account
Tenant signs up and completes identity verification.

## Step 11 — Tenant Views Personalized Equity Plan
Tenant sees:
- Monthly contribution options  
- Token Value  
- How Tokens are earned  
- Accrual schedule  
- Valuation cycle  

## Step 12 — Tenant Signs Agreements
Tenant executes:
- Standard Lease (unchanged)  
- TEPA (Token Agreement)

## Step 13 — Tenant Begins Earning Tokens
Automatic issuance based on:
- On-time rent  
- Monthly contributions  
- Maintenance tasks (if enabled)  
- Renewal  

## Step 14 — Monthly Statements
Tenant and landlord receive:
- Token summary  
- Property value changes  
- Incentive progress  
- Rent reporting status  

## Step 15 — Annual Valuation  
Token Value updates using appraisal/BPO/AVM.  
New equity is calculated.  
New Tokens may be minted.

---

# 3. Month-to-Month Operations

### Rent Paid → Tokens Issued  
Late rent may reduce or zero out Token accrual.

### Maintenance & Incentives  
If KeyPath sensors are used:
- Tenant receives alerts  
- Tenant performs quick tasks  
- Landlord avoids expensive repairs  
- Tenant may earn incentives  

### Renewal Success Fee  
If tenant renews, a performance fee is charged according to the pricing schedule.

---

# 4. Exit Scenarios  
### A. Tenant Moves Out  
Tenant receives:
- **Vested Token Value × Token Value**

Minus:
- Unpaid rent  
- Damages  
- Cleaning costs  

Unvested Tokens revert to landlord.

---

### B. Tenant Sells Tokens  
Tenant may:
- Sell to landlord (ROFR)  
- Sell to approved investors  
- Sell to KeyPath or partners if marketplace is enabled  

---

### C. Tenant Buys the Home  
When a tenant reaches a high ownership threshold (e.g., 80% of the Tenant Token Pool):
- They may pursue purchase  
- Tokens may convert to down payment credit  
- Traditional deed transfer is handled by a title company or attorney  

---

# 5. What Stays the Same for Landlords

- Landlord remains the **only** title holder  
- Landlord remains the **borrower** on the loan  
- Lender relationships remain unchanged  
- Property operations remain under landlord control  
- Eviction and rent rules remain under the Lease  

KeyPath is an overlay layer — not a replacement for traditional property operations.
