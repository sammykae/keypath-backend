Exit Scenarios
This file outlines all exit paths for both tenants and landlords.

1. Tenant Move-Out
Payout = Vested Tokens × Token Price at Last Valuation
Adjustments may include:
Outstanding rent
Confirmed damage charges
Cleaning fees
Unvested tokens are canceled.

2. Tenant-Initiated Sale
Tenant may sell tokens:
To landlord (first option)
To approved investors
Through marketplace
Via liquidity backstop at discount

3. Landlord Sale of Property
Tokens are valued:
At latest annual valuation
Or interim BPO/appraisal if required
Tenant receives payout from the economic proceeds.
Tokens do not block property sale.

4. Landlord Refinance
Refinance does not:
Dilute tenant tokens
Alter token pool percentage
Require tenant consent
Tenant economics remain intact.
