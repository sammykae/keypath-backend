Token Mechanics

1. Fixed Token Pool
Landlord allocates a fixed number of tokens derived from:
Property value
Landlord equity
Pool percentage
Tenant economics cannot exceed the defined pool.

2. Anti-Dilution
Token pool percentage remains fixed regardless of:
Refinancing
Capital improvements
Ownership restructuring

3. Accrual Methods
Tokens are earned through:
Rent
Incentives
Renewal bonuses
Contributions

4. Vesting Structure
Two categories:
Vested Tokens
Permanently earned.
Unvested Tokens
Contingent on Good Standing.

5. Buyback Rules
Tenants may sell tokens back:
At defined discount
At market rate (if investor available)
Through liquidity backstop
