Buyback and Liquidity Rules

1. Landlord Right of First Refusal
Before selling tokens externally, tenant must offer them to landlord.

2. Approved Investor Marketplace
Tokens may be sold to approved buyers within KeyPath ecosystem.

3. Liquidity Backstop
KeyPath may offer discounted liquidity if no buyer exists.

4. Exit Conversion
All tokens convert to cash value at:
Move-out
Sale of property
Buyout events
