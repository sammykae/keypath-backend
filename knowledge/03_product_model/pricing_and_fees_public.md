Pricing and Fees (Public Summary)
1. Landlord Fees
Onboarding fee (per unit)
Monthly per-unit software fee
Optional AI/analytics modules
Optional IoT sensors
2. Tenant Fees
One-time onboarding fee
Monthly participation fee
3. Renewal Success Fee
Landlord pays a percentage of monthly rent when a renewal is signed and paid.
This aligns incentives around tenant stability.
