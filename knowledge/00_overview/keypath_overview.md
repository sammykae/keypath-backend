KeyPath Overview

KeyPath was founded by Laurel Djoukeng in 2026. KeyPath is an AI-driven fintech platform that enables renters, landlords, and community stakeholders to participate in the economic value of residential real estate through tokenized equity.
KeyPath improves the housing system by:
Giving tenants a transparent, safe, and non-ownership path to build economic value while renting.
Helping landlords reduce turnover, increase NOI, and improve asset performance.
Supporting municipalities and community organizations with data transparency, improved resident stability, and tools to track outcomes.
KeyPath uses modern financial and technical infrastructure—including structured agreements, off-chain token ledgers, AI, OCR, and property-level analytics—to deliver a consistent, safe model for all participants.

Core Principles
Tokens Are Economic Rights Only
Tokens represent a claim on economic value, not ownership, title, governance, or control of the property.
Landlords Retain Full Control
KeyPath does not interfere with property management, leases, rent setting, operations, or lender relationships.
Tenant Safety and Transparency
The system prioritizes clarity, fairness, and standardized rules so tenants understand earnings, risks, and outcomes.
Legal and Policy Alignment
KeyPath is structured to avoid risks identified in historical rent-to-own programs, ensuring no disguised financing or predatory structures.
Data Integrity and Trust
All calculations—earnings, valuations, vesting, and cash-out events—occur through a transparent, auditable economic engine.
No Impact on Mortgage or Title
The ownership entity remains the same. Tokens do not modify deeds, create liens, or affect senior debt.
