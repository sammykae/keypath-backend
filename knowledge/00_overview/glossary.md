KeyPath Glossary
Token
A digital representation of economic value only associated with a specific property or pooled asset.
Tokens do not grant ownership, title, governance, voting rights, or occupancy rights.
Token Pool
A fixed set of economic value allocated for tenant participation. Created from the landlord’s existing equity.
Vested Tokens
Tokens fully earned by the tenant that remain theirs at exit, subject to payout rules.
Unvested Tokens
Tokens that require Good Standing and may be forfeited if the tenant violates major lease terms.
Good Standing
A compliance condition requiring:
On-time rent
No major lease violations
No material damage
No fraudulent activity
Accrual
The process of earning tokens through:
Rent payments
Incentives
Contributions
Annual Valuation
A property value assessment performed using appraisal, BPO, or AVM inputs. Determines the new token price.
Buyback
A process where the landlord, KeyPath, or approved investors purchase tokens from tenants under defined rules.
Liquidity Event
Any moment where the tenant can convert tokens into cash, including:
Move-out
Buyback
Marketplace sale (if applicable)
Refinance Safety Firewall
A rule ensuring that refinancing does not dilute tenant token value or alter token pool percentages.
Foreclosure Protection
A rule ensuring tenants receive the value of vested tokens in foreclosure scenarios before economic residuals are distributed.
Protected Series LLC
A legal structure that houses assets and token arrangements in liability-separated “series.”
