Legal FAQ
Is KeyPath a rent-to-own program?
No. Rent-to-own programs often involve ownership transfer obligations.
KeyPath provides economic participation only.

Is this a loan or financing product?
No. There is no credit extended to tenants, and no repayment obligations exist.

Does KeyPath create ownership rights?
No. Tokens do not create:
Ownership
Deed rights
Liens
Encumbrances

Is the landlord required to sell the property to the tenant?
No. TEPA provides no purchase obligation.

Are tokens securities?
Tokens represent contractual economic value in a limited context and are not intended to function as publicly tradable securities.
