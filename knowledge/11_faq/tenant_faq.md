Tenant FAQ
What is KeyPath?
KeyPath provides renters with the ability to earn economic value (tokens) based on their rental history and engagement. Tokens represent economic rights only, not ownership.

Do tokens give me ownership of my home?
No. Tokens are not ownership, title, or a pathway to automatic purchase.

How do I earn tokens?
Paying rent on time
Completing maintenance incentives
Lease renewals
Optional contributions

What happens if I pay rent late?
Late rent may:
Pause token accrual
Put unvested tokens at risk
Vested tokens remain yours.

What if I move out early?
You receive the value of your vested tokens, based on the most recent valuation.

Can my token value go down?
Yes. Token value depends on property valuation. If the property value declines, the token price also declines.
You never owe money if this occurs.

How do I cash out my tokens?
At move-out or via approved liquidity pathways:
Sell to landlord
Sell to approved investor
Marketplace (if available)
Liquidity backstop

Does KeyPath affect my rent?
No. Rent is determined solely by your landlord and your lease.

Does KeyPath affect my lease rights?
No. The lease continues to control habitability, rent, repairs, and eviction.

Does this help me buy a home?
Indirectly. Token value can support a down payment if you choose to purchase a property later.
