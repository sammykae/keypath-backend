Landlord FAQ
Does KeyPath change property ownership or title?
No. Landlords retain:
Full ownership
Full control
Full operational authority

Does this affect my mortgage or lender relationship?
No. Tokens do not modify:
Debt obligations
Covenants
Collateral structure

What is the benefit of offering tokens to tenants?
Key advantages:
Reduced turnover
Higher renewal rates
Improved maintenance behavior
Stronger tenant engagement

Does KeyPath take over property management?
No. All operations remain with the landlord or property manager.

How are valuations handled?
Through:
Appraisal
Broker Price Opinion
Automated Valuation Models
Blended methodology

What happens when I sell the property?
Tokens convert to cash value at the most recent valuation.
Tenants do not block or slow down a sale.

Does this increase my legal liability?
No. The program is structured to avoid additional liabilities and maintain clear role boundaries.
