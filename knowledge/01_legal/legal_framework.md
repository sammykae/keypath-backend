Legal Framework Overview
KeyPath structures token participation through contractual economic rights, not through property ownership transfers.

Key Legal Elements
1. Contract-Based Participation
Tenants receive rights defined solely by TEPA, not by real estate law governing ownership or mortgages.
2. Protected Series LLC Structure
Used to:
Isolate risk
Maintain clear recordkeeping
Separate token pools for each property or portfolio
3. Avoiding Regulated Financial Products
TEPA is designed to avoid:
Creating debt
Creating mortgage obligations
Triggering rent-to-own or installment sales rules
4. Compliance with Consumer Protection Standards
Documents are structured to avoid:
Ambiguous purchase obligations
Balloon payments
Hidden fees
Misaligned incentives
