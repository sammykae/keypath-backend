Risk Architecture Summary
KeyPath uses a multi-layered framework to protect tenants, landlords, lenders, and community partners.

1. Tenant-Facing Safeguards
No transfer of title or ownership
No rent-to-own conversion obligations
Clear Good Standing requirements
No predatory or disguised financing terms
Transparent valuations and accrual logic

2. Landlord-Facing Safeguards
TEPA does not limit eviction or enforcement
No impact on lender rights
No obligation to sell to tenant
Controlled token pool size
No open-ended liabilities

3. Lender-Facing Safeguards
Tokens operate outside the property’s ownership chain
No liens or encumbrances
No change to borrower or guarantor
No impairment of collateral

4. Municipal & Regulator-Facing Safeguards
Plain language disclosures
Standardized, repeatable structure
Compliance with consumer protection principles
Data transparency for outcomes measurement
