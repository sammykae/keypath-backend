Policy Alignment Summary
KeyPath aligns with guidance from:
Pew Charitable Trusts
National Consumer Law Center
State attorney general advisories
CFPB principles for consumer credit fairness

Historical Issues Avoided
KeyPath is intentionally designed to not replicate past rent-to-own problems such as:
Unclear ownership transfer
Excessive default penalties
Repairs shifted to tenants improperly
Disguised financing arrangements
Title risk exposure
KeyPath maintains:
Landlord repair obligations
Traditional lease requirements
No path to involuntary ownership
