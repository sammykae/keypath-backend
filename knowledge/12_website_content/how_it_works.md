How It Works — KeyPath Website
Step 1: Landlord Onboards Property
Equity and valuation verified; token pool defined.

Step 2: Tenants Sign TEPA
Clear documentation and plain-language summary provided.

Step 3: Tenants Earn Tokens Monthly
Through rent, incentives, and renewal events.

Step 4: Annual Valuation
Property value determines new token price.

Step 5: Cash-Out / Sell
Tenants convert vested tokens at move-out or liquidity events.
