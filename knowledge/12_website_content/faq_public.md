Public FAQ — KeyPath Website
What is KeyPath?
A platform enabling renters to earn economic participation while landlords improve retention and NOI.

Does this change my lease?
No. Lease terms remain the same.

Does this guarantee financial gains?
No. Token value depends on property performance.

Does KeyPath affect mortgage or title?
No. Landlord maintains full ownership and lender obligations.

How do I cash out?
Vested tokens convert to cash during move-out or approved liquidity events.
