Key Message
KeyPath enables tenants, landlords, investors, and community stakeholders to share in the economic value of residential real estate through structured, safe, transparent tokenization.

Problem Statement
Renters generate value in housing systems but historically have not participated economically.
Landlords face rising turnover and maintenance costs.
Communities lack tools for measuring housing stability.

Solution Overview
KeyPath provides:
Tenant equity tools
Landlord retention tools
Community dashboards
Safe, regulated-aligned token agreements

Outcomes
Stronger retention
Improved financial health for tenants
Better-performing housing assets
Data transparency for cities
