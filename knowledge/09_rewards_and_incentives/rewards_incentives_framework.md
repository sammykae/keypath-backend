Rewards and Incentives Framework
This file outlines how incentives improve tenant engagement and reduce maintenance.

1. Purpose of Incentives
- Encourage preventative maintenance
- Improve communication
- Reduce turnover and damage
- Support tenant Good Standing

2. Incentive Categories
- Routine cleaning
- Maintenance tasks
- Smoke detector checks
- HVAC filter replacement
- Lease renewal actions

3. Reward Structures
- Token bonuses
- Streak multipliers
- Completion badges
- Renewal incentives
