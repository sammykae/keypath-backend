Public-Private Partnership (PPP) Model Overview
KeyPath enables structured collaboration between landlords, municipalities, and mission-aligned organizations.

1. Shared Outcomes
Resident wealth-building
Reduced turnover
Increased community stability

2. Roles and Responsibilities
Private Sector:
Provide housing stock
Implement token program
Municipal Partners:
Supply land, subsidies, or grants
Support outreach
KeyPath:
Provide infrastructure
Ensure compliance
Manage token engine
